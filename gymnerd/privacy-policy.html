Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Not Bad Apps ("we", "us", "our") operates the GymNerd mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

GymNerd is built to work primarily offline. The workout data you enter — exercises, sets, reps, weights, and progress history — is stored locally on your device.

We may also collect limited technical data automatically, such as:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App diagnostics and crash reports
  • Anonymous usage analytics (e.g. feature usage, screen views)

2. How We Use Information

Any data collected is used only to:

  • Operate and improve the App's features and performance
  • Diagnose and fix crashes or bugs
  • Understand overall usage trends

We do not sell your data or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Data Storage and Backup

Your workout data stays on your device unless you explicitly export or back it up (e.g. via a file you choose to save or share). We do not have access to that data unless you send it to us directly, such as when contacting support.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (such as crash reporting or analytics providers) that collect information as described in this policy. These providers only receive anonymized or aggregated technical data, never your personal workout content.

5. Permissions

The App may request device permissions (such as notifications) solely to support in-app features like workout reminders. You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Since most data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the App. If you contacted us directly, you may request that we delete any information we hold about you.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
